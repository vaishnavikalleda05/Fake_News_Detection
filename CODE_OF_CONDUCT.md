# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The FactCheck AI Development Team is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all contributors, team members, and users. We value diversity and are dedicated to creating a community where everyone feels safe and respected.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful**: Treat all individuals with courtesy and respect
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives
- **Be Professional**: Maintain a professional tone in all communications
- **Be Collaborative**: Work together towards common goals
- **Be Honest**: Be truthful and transparent in your interactions
- **Be Accountable**: Take responsibility for your actions
- **Be Open**: Listen to feedback and be willing to learn from others

### Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Discrimination based on race, color, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, or disability
- Offensive or derogatory language or comments
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate conduct
- Sharing confidential information
- Deliberate misinformation or bad-faith arguments
- Retaliation against anyone reporting violations

---

## Reporting Guidelines

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document the Incident**
   - Note the date, time, and details
   - Record any relevant communications

2. **Report Privately**
   - Contact the team leads/maintainers directly
   - Email: [team contact email]
   - Do not post publicly about conflicts or concerns

3. **Provide Information**
   - Describe the incident clearly
   - Include relevant context
   - Suggest resolution if appropriate

---

## Enforcement

### Investigation Process

1. Reports will be reviewed within 48 hours
2. We will investigate thoroughly and confidentially
3. All parties involved will be interviewed
4. We will gather relevant evidence

### Consequences

Actions taken may include (in order of severity):

1. **Warning** - For minor violations
   - Private discussion with the person
   - Clarification of expectations
   
2. **Temporary Restriction** - For moderate violations
   - Temporary suspension from contributions
   - Required to complete training or reflection

3. **Removal** - For serious or repeated violations
   - Permanent removal from team or project
   - Possible additional actions if appropriate

All enforcement actions are at the discretion of the leadership team and will be documented.

---

## Confidentiality

- All reports will be kept confidential
- Details will only be shared with those who need to know
- Reporters and respondents have the right to privacy
- We will not disclose reporter identity without consent (except when legally required)

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All team members and contributors
- Project communications (GitHub, email, Slack, etc.)
- Code reviews and discussions
- Team meetings and events
- Public representations of the project
